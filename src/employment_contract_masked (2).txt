FICTIONAL EMPLOYMENT CONTRACT
TEST DOCUMENT ONLY — NOT A REAL CONTRACT OR LEGAL ADVICE. This fictional example is for software
testing. It has not been checked for compliance with Norwegian law and must not be used as a real employment
agreement.
This sample is written in English and includes simple example clauses so a document-processing system can
test questions about salary, working hours, leave, notice, and privacy.
1. Parties
Employer: Fjord Example Technology AS (fictional company).
Business address: 10 Example Street, Oslo, Norway (fictional address).
Employee: <PERSON> (fictional person).
Position: Junior Software Developer.
2. Start Date and Employment Type
The proposed start date is 1 November 2026. This sample describes a full-time, ongoing position. These details
are illustrative only and have not been assessed against applicable legal requirements.
3. Salary and Payment
The example gross salary is NOK 35,000 per month before tax and other lawful deductions. Salary is intended
to be paid monthly, on the 25th day of each month, to the employee's nominated bank account. Any overtime
payment, supplements, deductions, or other compensation would need to be handled according to the actual
agreement and applicable rules.
4. Working Hours
The example normal working schedule is 37.5 hours per week, Monday to Friday. The usual daily schedule is
09:00 to 17:00, including a one-hour unpaid lunch break. This clause is included for testing and is not a
statement that the schedule satisfies all legal requirements.
5. Work Location
The normal work location is the employer's fictional office in Oslo. Remote work may be agreed in writing
between the employer and employee.
6. Holiday and Leave
The employee's holiday and leave arrangements will be handled in accordance with the applicable agreement
and relevant Norwegian rules. This sample does not specify a complete holiday entitlement or holiday-pay
calculation.
7. Sickness Absence
The employee should notify the employer as soon as reasonably possible when unable to work due to illness.
Documentation and payment during sickness absence are subject to applicable requirements and procedures.
8. Confidentiality and Personal Data
The employee must handle confidential business information responsibly. The employer should explain what
personal data it processes, why it processes that data, how long it is retained, and how the employee can
exercise applicable data-protection rights. This clause is intentionally general and requires review before any
real use.
9. Overtime
Overtime should be agreed or assigned only when permitted by applicable rules. The employer and employee
must confirm the applicable overtime arrangements and compensation before relying on this clause.
